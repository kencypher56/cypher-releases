## What's new in 1.4.1

- **Pull down no longer spins for ever.** With no connection, the refresh used to fail so fast that the spinner never stopped. Now it stops as soon as the check is over, and the line under the title says Offline, with Retry.
- It is signed with the same key as every store before it, so it installs over the one you have and keeps your settings.
